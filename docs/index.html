The idea

Travel is full of people you were meant to meet

You're rarely the only one on your flight, at your conference, or on that beach with kids the same age as yours. GateMate finds those people from one simple thing: your trip. Anyone who travels can use it — solo flyers, couples, families, friends, business travelers.

The new concept

Your trip is the heart of everything

We rebuilt the app around one object: the trip. You tell GateMate where you're going — everything else is derived from it automatically.

1

Add a trip, any way you like

With a flight number, with just a destination and dates — or in "Still planning" mode when nothing is booked yet and you want people to plan with.

2

GateMate derives your shared contexts

Each trip places you in up to three circles of people, from most to least specific:

✈️ Everyone on your flight 🎟️ Everyone at your event 📍 Everyone at your destination on your dates
3

The trip hub shows who's there

One screen per trip, one section per circle: matched travelers with the reasons they match ("Same flight", "Kids same age", "Still planning"), and the groups living in each circle.

4

Groups belong to the circle, not to a person

Open "Taxi from the airport" on your flight and every traveler on that flight sees it — whoever they are, whenever they add their trip. Join a group and the chat opens.

Before & after

What changed

Before

Groups and people hung off one person's trip. A taxi group was invisible to the very passengers it was meant for, and matching needed an exact overlap — so a new app felt empty.

Now

Groups live in shared circles everyone's trip can reach. And while the community grows, discovery is wide by default: same origin or shared interests is enough to see each other. Each person can switch to focused matching in their profile.
